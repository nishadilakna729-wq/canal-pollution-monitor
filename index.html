Understanding Water Indices

MNDWI — Modified Normalized Difference Water Index

Detects open water bodies and distinguishes water from built-up urban areas. Higher values indicate the presence of water surfaces like canals, lakes, and flooded areas.

Positive values (+) = Water present  |  Negative (-) = Dry land / Urban
NDWI — Normalized Difference Water Index

Measures vegetation water content and moisture levels. Useful for identifying areas where vegetation near canals has adequate water or is under drought stress.

Positive (+) = Healthy vegetation moisture  |  Negative (-) = Dry / Stressed vegetation
NDTI — Normalized Difference Turbidity Index

Indicates water turbidity and pollution level. High turbidity often signals suspended sediments, sewage discharge, or industrial effluent in the canal water.

High values = Turbid / Polluted water  |  Low values = Clear water
