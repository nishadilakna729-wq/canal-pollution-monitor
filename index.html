What the Map Colours Show

💧
NDWI — Surface Water Detection Highlights surface water bodies using Green & NIR bands. Positive values (+) indicate water is present. Negative values (−) indicate land surface.
🏙️
MNDWI — Urban Water Mapping Better than NDWI for separating water from built-up urban areas using Green & SWIR bands. Positive values (+) indicate water. Especially useful for detecting canals in cities.
🟤
NDTI — Water Turbidity Estimates how cloudy or sediment-loaded the water is using Red & Green bands. Higher positive values suggest more turbid (dirtier) water. Negative values suggest clearer water.
